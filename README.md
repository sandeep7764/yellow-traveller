# 🚐 Yellow Traveller: Endless Forest Road Trip

> **🎮 Play the Game Live:** [https://sandeep7764.github.io/yellow-traveller/](https://sandeep7764.github.io/yellow-traveller/)

**Yellow Traveller** is an immersive 3D endless forest driving and survival overland game built with **Three.js** and **Tailwind CSS**. Navigate a winding pine highway through day and night, manage your fuel and fatigue, haul courier cargo between 10km towns, and upgrade your expedition fleet!

---

## ✨ Key Features

1. **Endless Forest Highway & Dynamic Day/Night Cycle**:
   - Drive along a procedural forest highway lined with pine trees, wooden perimeter fences, boulders, and roadside coins.
   - Smooth natural day/night cycle (or instant toggle via **`T`** in Casual Mode), bringing out glowing green-yellow fireflies drifting through the trees at night and working car headlights & taillights.
2. **Yellow Traveller Station Wagon & 3D First/Third-Person Cameras**:
   - Custom 3D station wagon equipped with woodgrain side panels and a roof rack carrying vintage suitcases, a red jerrycan, a rolled tent, a spare tire, and delivery cargo crates.
   - Press **`C`** to toggle between **Third-Person Chase View** and **First-Person Cockpit View** (complete with an animated steering wheel and glowing dashboard).
3. **Rest & Drowsiness Survival Mechanics**:
   - As your **Fatigue Meter** fills past 75%, drowsiness vignette effects and steering drift kick in. Reaching 100% triggers a punishing **Pass Out / Ranger Tow** penalty.
   - Rest anywhere on the highway using a **Car Nap (`N`)** (-35% fatigue) or step inside the cozy furnished cabin at **The Timber Lodge** in any 10km Town to sleep in a warm bed for 6 hours (`-100%` fatigue + **Well-Rested Speed Buff**).
4. **10 km Town Plazas, Quests & Dealerships**:
   - Every **10 km**, a roadside forest town generates featuring **The Timber Lodge**, **The Firefly Bar & Diner**, **The Gas Station**, and an **Auto Dealership**.
   - Stop your car inside the **Highlighted Yellow Town Parking Bay** to check in.
   - Talk to NPCs like **Dispatcher Eli `[!]`** and **Quartermaster Clara `★`** in first-person with typewriter subtitles and procedural voice blips to accept and complete courier delivery contracts.
   - **Missed Town Delivery Fail System**: Driving past your target delivery town triggers a penalty (-Coins) and a "DELIVERY FAILED" alert.
5. **Vehicles & Upgrades (`U`)**:
   - **Starter Yellow Traveller Wagon**: Max Speed 85 km/h (Upgradable to Level 5).
   - **Pinecrest Outback 4x4** (650 ₵): Max Speed 120 km/h (Upgradable to Level 10).
   - **Timberline Rally GT** (1140 ₵): Max Speed 160 km/h (Upgradable to Level 15).
   - **Firefly V8 Cyber Nomad** (1720 ₵): Max Speed 205 km/h (Upgradable to Level 20).
6. **Dynamic Weather, Traffic & Car Radio (`R`)**:
   - Press **`V`** to switch between **Clear**, **Thick Forest Mist**, and **Rainstorm** (with animated 3D windshield wipers).
   - Watch out for oncoming traffic strictly in the left lane and glowing-eyed forest deer crossing the highway at night!
   - Press **`R`** to cycle through the procedural multi-station **Car Radio** (Synthwave, Acoustic Campfire, Lo-Fi Beats, and OFF).

## ⌨️ Controls Guide

| Key / Action | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`W` / `S` or `▲` / `▼`** | Accelerate / Drive Forward & Reverse (or walk on foot) |
| **`A` / `D` or `◀` / `▶`** | Steer Left / Right (or turn walking avatar) |
| **`F`** | Universal Interaction: Exit/Enter Car, talk to NPCs, open Town doors |
| **`C`** | Switch between 3rd-Person Chase Cam & 1st-Person Cockpit View |
| **`R`** | Cycle Car Radio Stations (Synthwave, Acoustic, Lo-Fi, OFF) |
| **`N`** | Quick Car Nap (-35% Fatigue from anywhere on highway) |
| **`T`** | Toggle Day / Night (Casual Mode only) |
| **`V`** | Cycle Weather: Clear / Mist / Rain (Casual Mode only) |
| **`I`** | Open Backpack & Cargo Inventory (Thermos Coffee, Trail Meals, Jerrycans) |
| **`U`** | Open Town Garage & Dealership (Available inside 10km Towns) |
| **`P`** | Pause Game / Options Menu |
| **`ESC`** | Close active modals & dialogues |

---

## 🎮 Game Modes

- **Casual Mode**: Allows manual time toggles (`[T]`) and weather switches (`[V]`) anytime.
- **Survival Mode**: Pure hardcore overland challenge where time and weather cycles are strictly locked to natural progression!
